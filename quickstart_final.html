🔄 Working together: Refresh

Several recruiters can edit the same list at the same time. Here is how to stay in sync.
Your screen is your own copy. When a colleague saves, your browser does not necessarily update right away. Press 🔄 Refresh to fetch the latest shared list and merge it with your work.

🟢 Press 🔄 Refresh…

  • when you start working
  • before relying on a colleague's status ("already contacted?")
  • when a colleague says "I just updated the list"
  • if something looks out of date
  • before you finish

🥇 4 golden rules

  1. 🔄 Refresh when you start, then edit.
  2. Wait for ✅ All changes saved before you close the tab or press 🔄 Refresh.
  3. Split the work (e.g. by alphabet) if two of you edit at once.
  4. Conflict message or banner "Another recruiter made changes…": save, then 🔄 Refresh, and check the final checkboxes.

🔀 What the merge does

SituationResult
Different candidates✅ both kept
Same candidate, different boxes✅ both kept
Same box, same value✅ fine
Same box, different values⚠️ your value wins + message
💡 Refresh never replaces your own unsaved work, but it is not a save button either.
